## Behavior and boundaries

An attested `source-interaction-look-v1` artifact binds the selected heroes to
their current definition hashes. A supported committed A1 interaction uses its
existing source parent, physical kernel, ordinary cycle and `Store.once` receipt.
It looks at the actual top copy without drawing it, awards one inspiration for
printed fate with the existing cap, and resumes the original source once. Later
choices, messages, inspection, reader close and retries do not trigger again.
A second genuine interaction in the same round can trigger again.

The saved before window records remaining and completed effects. The party can
choose material cost/look order: at six inspiration and cap six, look then spend
one ends at five; spend then look ends at six. All applicable faceup damage and
fear obligations are included. Guaranteed resources can finance a safe order;
an unrevealed conditional reward cannot finance a mandatory obligation.

Ready provoking groups are chosen by the hero. Exhausted groups are excluded.
The supported plan completes the actual enemy children before ordered costs/look
and source return. The UI also identifies the unsupported early-look plan and
explains its gate. Completed consequences are retained.

These combinations remain capability gates, not claims about original rules:

- Mandatory obligations that require funding from an unrevealed fate reward.
- Looking first when a later provocation can interrupt the interaction; the
  continuation ruling is still unresolved.
- A genuinely empty deck and discard; held or prepared cards are not borrowed.
- Missing or unknown active before-interaction capability, and A2 interactions.
