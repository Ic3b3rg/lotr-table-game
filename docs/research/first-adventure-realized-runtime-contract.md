**Implementation decision:** this realization adds **one encounter placement acknowledgement before objective 0**: encounter 5's threat token on 302A. Encounter 123, also assigned to 302A, stays hidden during automatic exploration. Its reveal depends on encounter 119 in section 3. These findings remove the unknown generated-node dependency for this realization; they do not implement startup or complete #38. **E — Lifecycle order is consequential.** `SpawnEncounter` first calls `Instantiate`, then assigns world position, map parent, `space.Node`, `node.Space` and `EncounterId`, in that order. Node.Awake calls Initialize: cache the FSM/graphical child, disable its renderer/collider, initialize the tween, and set visible=false. PlayMakerFSM.Awake initializes globals and loads the FSM; P.Fsm.Awake invokes Init and Awake on **every action in every state**, not just the start state. The 31 selected action classes and their ancestor chains were checked: their Init resolves owner/FSM references; their inherited Awake/OnPreprocess are empty, and their constructors introduce no gameplay request or broadcast. The static player-choice texture array also creates no request. [A `0x06001334 IL_0000–005c`, `0x06001599/159c`; P `0x0600004e/0050/0451/05f3/05f7/05f8`; selected action constructors and inheritance in `action-owners.json`.]

**E — OnEnable is not action entry on a fresh instance.** It registers the FSM and selects its start state; first Start calls Fsm.Start, which enters the state. All selected FSMs have `RestartOnEnable=true`, `ResetVariablesOnEnable=false`, and non-sequential states. After the spawn loop, the builder yields 0.1 seconds and later another frame before completion; the loader then completes its transition before `BeginAdventure(false)`. Thus the fort's entry actions can bind the now-assigned Space/Transform before setup. The generic scene reset in BeginAdventure resets hero count, enemy points, round, threat, darkness and its override, **not the fort bindings**. No selected entry path presents a popup, spawns an enemy or emits a group event. [P `0x06000058/0059/0458/045c`; A `0x06003c93 IL_1961–197a,19fa–1a1e`, `0x06003932 IL_04c5–053e`, `0x06000b8a/0ca5/0ce1`, R27686.s[2].]
